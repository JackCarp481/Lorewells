### 6 · How It Works  — *reduce process uncertainty*
- **Eyebrow:** `§ 05 — HOW IT WORKS`
- **Headline:** How a story is preserved.
- **Steps (mono-numbered, restrained — DF §8):**
  1. **Nominate or sponsor** a participant.
  2. **Schedule** the interview.
  3. **Film** the long-form Lorewells interview — 2–4 hours, multi-camera.
  4. **Edit, transcribe, and preserve.**
  5. **Deliver** to the family and the archive.
- *Source: §6 + §4.5.*
