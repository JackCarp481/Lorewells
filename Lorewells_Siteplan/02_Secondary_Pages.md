## ⭐ `/organizations` — For Organizations (the org close)

**Goal:** convert a veterans group / first-responder org / historical society / church / foundation into a **partner inquiry** or a **funded pilot**. Confident, civic, fundable register.

| # | Section | Content / source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | [DRAFT] *"Honor them while they're still here."* Sub: preserve your members' stories and give your community a permanent archive. CTA: **[ Start the conversation ]** + secondary **[ Download the proposal (PDF) ]** (§7). |
| 2 | **The opportunity** | Why now — the last living witnesses are aging (§3.1). Short, urgent. |
| 3 | **Who this is for** | Veterans orgs · first-responder nonprofits · historical societies · churches · community foundations · hospice (§4.3, §4.4). |
| 4 | **The partnership model** | The interview model + production quality (§4.5): 2–4 hrs, 4K multi-cam, documentary edit. |
| 5 | **The three tiers** | $5K / **$30K pilot ◆** / $75K (§4.11). Middle emphasized (DF §7). Each: who it's for + plain-language deliverables + CTA. |
| 6 | **What your organization receives** | Archive materials to honor members, educate, preserve local history; org archive listing; optional launch event/screening (§4.4, §4.10 Organization Archive). |
| 7 | **`#where-it-goes` — Where your gift goes** | The budget breakdown (§4.13) as a transparency block: *"Of a $5,000 sponsorship: ~$1,500 production day · $1,750 editing/post · $500 audio-color-export · $300 transcript · $250 archival · $500 pre-pro · $200 admin."* 501(c)(3) trust line. **This block does heavy conversion work — make it prominent.** |
| 8 | **The recommended first step** | The Central Oregon Veterans Pilot (§4.10): 6 veterans, $30K, deliverables list, "why this" rationale. |
| 9 | **Ownership & access** | Private / organization / public / hybrid models, plainly (§4.8) — answers the "who controls it" question. |
| 10 | **Ethics** | Consent, dignity, boundaries, historical humility (§4.14) — reassurance for institutional partners. |
| 11 | **Partner inquiry form** | Org, role, # members, tier interest, message → success state (see `03_Content_Map_and_Forms.md`). |

**Primary CTA:** Partner inquiry. **Secondary:** download one-page proposal PDF (built from §7), fund a tier directly via `/give`.

## ⭐ `/families` — For Families (the family close)

**Goal:** convert an adult child / grandchild into a **preserve-a-story inquiry** (or a direct $5,000 gift). Warm, unhurried, low-pressure register — the emotional opposite of the org page.

| # | Section | Content / source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | [DRAFT] *"Preserve his story while he can still tell it."* Sub: one day your children will want to hear your father's voice — we help you keep it. CTA: **[ Preserve a story ]**. Warm key-light portrait. |
| 2 | **The ache** | [WRITE] The after-the-funeral regret, gently (§3.10): *"Families often don't realize what they've lost until the questions come — and there's no one left to ask."* |
| 3 | **What we preserve** | The 10 interview categories as themes, softened (§4.6): origins, childhood, family history, work, love, hardship, faith, wisdom for the grandchildren. |
| 4 | **How it works for families** | The 5 steps in a gentle register (§4.5/§6). Reassure: *we guide the whole way; your loved one stays in control of what they share.* |
| 5 | **What your family receives** | Full film, transcript, private family link, highlight film, archive copy (§4.7). |
| 6 | **Handled with reverence** | Consent, boundaries, dignity — never extract trauma, always in their control (§4.14). Critical trust block for families. |
| 7 | **The gift** | $5,000 framed as a gift, not a purchase (501(c)(3)). *"A sponsored Lorewells interview — the whole life story, preserved."* |
| 8 | **Family inquiry form** | Your name, relationship, who you'd preserve, why now → gentle success state. |

**Primary CTA:** Preserve-a-story inquiry. **Secondary:** Nominate someone (if not ready to commit), direct gift via `/give`.

## `/why-it-matters` — Why It Matters (funder credibility)

**Goal:** give funders and thoughtful donors the research backbone. Editorial long-read.

- **Hero:** [DRAFT] *"This is more than video. It's memory, identity, and history."* (§3 intro)
- **Sections (condensed from §3, statistic-as-language not stat-blocks):**
  - The memory crisis — more data, less memory (§3.1)
  - Why family stories matter — Duke & Fivush, the "Do You Know?" scale, the intergenerational self (§3.2)
  - Narrative identity — McAdams (§3.3)
  - Oral history as preservation — Smithsonian, Oral History Association (§3.4)
  - Life review & well-being in elders (§3.5)
  - Story, trauma, and meaning — veterans/first responders, *reverence not extraction* (§3.6)
  - The dignity of elder wisdom (§3.7)
  - The future value of recorded memory (§3.9)
- **Reference notes** at the foot (§9) — signals rigor. Frame all as *firsthand testimony*, with the memory-vs-verified-history caveat (§4.14).
- **CTA:** → both doors.
